Privacy Policy

Crown Games • Last Updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Crown Games, we prioritize keeping your layout clean and fast. We do not require you to create an account or sign up to play. As a result, we do not collect personal identifiers like your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We may use local data parameters (like browser LocalStorage) solely to remember your preferences, saved game configurations, or high scores locally on your machine. This data remains completely on your device.

3. Third-Party Scripts

Our platform loads open-source game assets via content delivery networks (CDNs). These third-party delivery elements may automatically handle standard backend web requests (such as your IP address or browser type) essential to transfer network files to your browser.

4. Changes to This Policy

Crown Games reserves the right to modify this statement as functionality grows. Any layout updates or feature upgrades to how storage variables are stored will be directly updated on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy setup, please contact the site administration panel.
